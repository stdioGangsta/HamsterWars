How to play

Teams take turns. On your turn, move one hamster, pick a weapon and fire before the clock runs out. Knock enemies into the water or drain their health to zero.

←→Walk (on rope / jet pack: swing / steer)
↑↓Aim (on rope: climb up / down · jet pack: thrust · girder: rotate)
SpaceFire. Hold to charge power for throwing & launching weapons, release to fire
EnterJump forward (also lets go of the rope / jet pack)
BackspaceBackflip (high jump)
W or right-clickOpen the weapon menu
1–5Set the fuse timer on grenades and other timed weapons
TabSwitch to another hamster (before you've moved)
MouseClick to choose targets (air strike, homing, teleport, girder). Drag to look around, wheel to zoom
Esc · M · HPause · sound on/off · show or hide the key hints

🌐 Online: tap Online on the main menu. One player hosts and shares the 5-letter code; friends join with it and each control their own team on their own device. The host's device runs the match, so the host should keep the game open and the screen on.

📱 On a phone or tablet: use the on-screen buttons. ◀ ▶ walk, ▲ ▼ aim, hold FIRE to charge, JUMP / FLIP to hop, 🎒 for weapons, ⏱ for the fuse, ⇄ to switch hamster. Tap the map to pick targets, drag with one finger to look around, pinch to zoom. Landscape works best.

Tips: wind (bottom right) pushes rockets and air strikes. Grenades ignore the wind. After you attack you get a few seconds to run. Hurting yourself ends your turn. Crates drop in by parachute — walk into them for health or extra weapons. Watch out for mines and barrels!
